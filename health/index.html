BigPic Health

A personal, single-user project

BigPic Health is a private health game built by and for one person, Daryll Gomas of Big Pic Solutions, Portland, Oregon. It reads his own activity, sleep and heart-rate data from his own Fitbit through the Google Health API, turns it into daily quests and a small role-playing game, and sends gentle nudges to his own watch.

It is not a product or a public service, and no one else can sign in to it.
