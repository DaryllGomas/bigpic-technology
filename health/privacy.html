Privacy Policy — BigPic Health

Last updated 28 September 2026

The short version. One person reads his own health data, read-only, onto his own home server, to power his own private game. Nothing is shared, sold, advertised against, or used to train anything.

What this covers

This policy covers the Google OAuth client BigPic Health (Google Cloud project bigpic-health), operated by Daryll Gomas of Big Pic Solutions, Portland, Oregon.

What is accessed

Three read-only scopes are requested:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/googlehealth.activity_and_fitness.readonly — steps, active minutes, floors, workouts.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/googlehealth.health_metrics_and_measurements.readonly — resting heart rate and heart-rate variability.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/googlehealth.sleep.readonly — sleep sessions and minutes asleep.

All three are read-only. This client cannot write, change or delete any health data.

Where the data goes

A collector on the operator’s own home server, on his private network, reads these numbers every 15 minutes and keeps them there, together with the OAuth token. They are used to build the day’s quests and game state, which are shown on his own watch and in his own browser.

No copy is sent to Big Pic Solutions as a business, to any analytics provider, advertiser, data broker or other third party. Nothing is sold, rented, or used to train models. There is no tracking and no profiling.

Who can use this client

One person: the operator, signing in with his own Google account. It is not offered to the public.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept on the operator’s server until he deletes it. Deleting the game’s data folder removes the stored numbers and the token completely.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking invalidates the stored token and the collector simply stops.

Limited Use

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through this client is used solely to run the operator’s own private health game, and is never transferred to others, used for advertising, or read by anyone other than the operator.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revision date above changes with it.
